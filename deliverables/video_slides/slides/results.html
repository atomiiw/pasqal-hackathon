Share of reads that give the best plan: fifty-one to ninety-one percent on small, thirteen to twenty on medium, two to thirty-two on large. Against random bits, decoded the same way: two to four times better on small, seven to twelve on medium, over sixty on large. Against a stricter comparison, random anchors: even on medium, up to eight times on large. The honest price: on medium and large, only a third to a half of the reads are usable; the rest we throw away.
